## Prompt set

- Mika: semi-realistic anime companion portrait in a midnight bedroom, lavender knit sweater, warm peach bedside light, calm reassuring expression.
- Luna: silver-haired patient listener with a white cat, cool moonlight and soft lavender palette.
- Aiden: black-haired rational companion in a dark knit sweater, composed expression and restrained warm rim light.
- Night background: starry navy bedroom window, crescent moon, sleeping gray-and-white cat, warm lamp and dark negative space.
- Onboarding: reflective adult woman by a moonlit city window, sleeping cat, warm moon lamp and generous headline space.

The generated PNG masters were resized with high-quality bicubic interpolation and exported at JPEG quality 88 to keep the complete cross-platform asset set below the WeChat Mini Program main-package limit.
