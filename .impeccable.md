## Design Context

### Users
HeartNest serves people who want a low-pressure place to express their feelings, talk with a companion, and revisit their emotional history. The main experience is mobile, including WeChat Mini Program, H5, and App.

### Brand Personality
Gentle, reassuring, intimate. Help people feel welcome without grading their emotions or pressuring them to maintain a streak.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the confirmed HeartNest specification in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-heartnest-cross-platform-design.md`: midnight blue scenery, restrained translucent surfaces, soft rose and violet accents, and warm highlights. Preserve the existing brand atmosphere when refining an established page. Aim for readable mobile layouts and reliable native controls across platforms.

### Design Principles
1. Put personal feelings and useful next steps ahead of decorative metrics.
2. Make primary text readable and give controls sufficient space.
3. Use gentle, specific copy; distinguish unrecorded days from recorded feelings.
4. Apply essential layout to native elements inside custom components so WeChat and H5 behave consistently.
5. Keep loading, failure, empty, and filtered states clear and recoverable.
